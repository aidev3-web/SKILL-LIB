read **`<skill-root>/assets/research-rules.md`** (shared

## Sections you own

`competitive-intel`, `top3-competitor-deep-dive`, `pricing-strategy`,
`regional-expansion`, `advisory`. This is the complete list for this group — see
`assets/menu-structure.md` for the full sidebar. **You no longer write
`sources-citation` by hand** — it's generated by a script from the merged
`<client-slug>-findings.json` during `source-auditor`'s pass (it's just a formatted
list of every citation already used elsewhere in the file, nothing to research). The
`cRisk`/`cKpi`/`cRoi` charts and the Mutual Action Plan table live in the
orchestrator's `recommendations` section, not here.

## Chart you own

`cOwner` (bubble, on `advisory`) — the major recommended decisions, impact vs. effort.

## What to research and write

Reason about growth strategy, pricing strategy, regional expansion, and risk factors
for this client (this reasoning still happens — it feeds the orchestrator's
`recommendations` section via your digest instead of a Risk Register/RACI section of
your own). Use `competitor-research/*.json` (see above) for `top3-competitor-deep-dive`:
- **Messaging Comparison Matrix + Content Gap Analysis** inside
  `top3-competitor-deep-dive` — a `.tbl` with one row per messaging theme (e.g.
  "price", "speed of service") and one column per competitor plus this client,
  showing who claims what — built from the shared competitor-research files, not new
  research of your own.
- **`advisory`** (with the `cOwner` chart) — the major recommended decisions this
  client should make, impact vs. effort.
- **`pricing-strategy`**, **`regional-expansion`**, **`competitive-intel`** —
  concrete to this client, not generic strategy language.
- In your digest, surface the sharpest 4–8 risks you found (module dependency risk,
  change-resistance, competitive risk, etc.) — the orchestrator's `recommendations`
  section pulls these into its `cRisk` chart instead of a separate Risk Register.

**Self-check each section as you finish it, not all at the end** — after writing each
of the 5 sections, immediately verify it against `research-rules.md`'s citation/
`.assess` rules before moving to the next one, rather than writing all 5 and only then
running one big check at the end (that tail-loaded check is what made a past run of
this group finish noticeably later than the others).

## Output

Return: the `<section id="...">` fragments for the 5 slugs above with the `cOwner`
chart built in, a digest that includes both your section content AND the risk/growth
reasoning that feeds `recommendations`. Write your citations to **your own
`<client-slug>-findings-groupC.json`** — never a shared `<client-slug>-findings.json`
(a real race condition when 4 groups append in parallel; `source-auditor` merges the 4
group files later, and generates `sources-citation` from that merge). Save your
section fragments to `<client-slug>-p1-groupC.html` as **raw fragments concatenated
together, not a full template copy** — the orchestrator/`checkpoint-manager`
mechanically stitches these into a real previewable full-page copy afterward.
